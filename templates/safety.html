How SOS works

Tapping the 🆘 SOS button in the top bar opens your phone's messaging app with a message already written — your name, your emergency contact, and your next ride's route and time if you have one. If you allow location access, a Google Maps link to your current position is added too. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself, and CommuteSync's server never sends this message — your phone does.
